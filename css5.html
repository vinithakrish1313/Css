Chrunchyroll

Crunchyroll is the world's leading dedicated anime streaming service, owned by Sony Group Corporation (joint venture of Sony Pictures Entertainment and Aniplex). It offers a massive library of over 1,000 anime titles, simulcasts, and Crunchyroll Originals, alongside merchandise, games, and news. Primarily, it serves a global audience in 200+ countries with subtitles and dubs.

Crunchyroll allows users to stream anime anytime, anywhere on various devices, including smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, and smart TVs. Premium members enjoy higher-quality streaming and faster access to new episodes.
